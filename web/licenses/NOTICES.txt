Language data (all versions)
----------------------------
Dictionaries: GiellaLT dict-sme-fin, dict-sme-nob, dict-sme-eng,
dict-sme-smn, dict-sme-smj, dict-sme-sma, dict-fin-sme and dict-nob-sme
  © GiellaLT / Divvun / Giellatekno, UiT The Arctic University of Norway,
  and contributors.
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  (CC-BY-4.0.txt, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt
  Changes: the dictionaries were merged into one database and extended
  with words recognised by the North Sámi FST, inflection tables,
  consonant gradation and hand-made corrections (korjaukset.tsv in the
  source code); Norwegian letters in Finnish translations were fixed.

North Sámi morphology: GiellaLT lang-sme
  Authors: Linda Wiechetek, Katri Hiovain-Asikainen, Inga Lill Sigga
  Mikkelsen, Sjur N. Moshagen, Flammie A Pirinen, Trond Trosterud,
  Børre Gaup and the lang-sme contributors.
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-sme
  The inflection tables were generated with it.

Finnish inflection: GiellaLT lang-fin, based on the Omorfi lexicon
(Tommi A. Pirinen and others)
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-fin

Norwegian inflection: Norsk ordbank – bokmål 2005 (dump of 2022-02-01)
  © Språkbanken / Nasjonalbiblioteket
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0.txt)
  Source: https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/ressurskatalog/oai-nb-no-sbr-5/
  Changes: only the forms of words used as translations are kept;
  compounds missing from the word bank are inflected by their last part.

Consonant gradation groups: Oahpa (Giellatekno, UiT)
  https://oahpa.no/sme/gramm/tabell_stadieveksling.html

Pronunciation: speech synthesis of Divvun and Giellatekno (UiT The Arctic
University of Norway), voices Biret, Máhtte and Sunná
  Used through the network service api-giellalt.uit.no with Divvun's
  permission. The voice samples in voices/ were generated with the same
  service and are included with Divvun's permission.

Logo: the Sámi flag (designed by Astrid Båhl, 1986) drawn as a drop.
